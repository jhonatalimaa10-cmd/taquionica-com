Machine retrofit: the mechanics still work, the electronics don't have to stay old

When a machine is mechanically sound but its controls are obsolete, unavailable or unreliable, we redesign the electronics and control around it, and connect it to the rest of the plant.

Get a quote What's included

When companies call us

If one of these sounds familiar, this is the service to start with.

  • Spare parts are no longer availableA failed board can stop the machine for weeks.
  • Unexplained stopsThe control fails intermittently and nobody can find the cause.
  • No way to collect dataThe machine produces, but can't report what it is doing.
  • A replacement costs far more than the problemBuying a new machine to fix a control issue doesn't add up.
